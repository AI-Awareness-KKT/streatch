Start your camera, or try the illustrated demo with your mouse. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

01Grab a feature

Face the camera and check that the green contour follows your forehead, cheeks and chin. Pinch near your face to pull it. Choose Whole head to include hair and ears.

02Choose your kind of silly

Stretch to pull, Inflate to grow, Shrink to squeeze, or Twist to swirl. Move sideways for Twist. Try Big head for an instant look.

03Keep adding stretches

Turn on Keep shape, pull and release, then grab another feature. Each stretch stays. Undo last removes one, and Reset face clears them all.
